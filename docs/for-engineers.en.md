# Engineer Track

For CTOs, Heads of AI Platform and developers. BTV is a gateway: swap your
agent's `OPENAI_BASE_URL` and every LLM call is intercepted, validated and
audited — without modifying the agent's code.

1. **[Quickstart](./quickstart.md)** — bring up the stack and make the first governed call.
